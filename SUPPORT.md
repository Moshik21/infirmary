# Support

Support is through GitHub issues on this repository. The project is maintained in spare time and is advice-only by default, so the
answers below come first. It is best effort, with no promised response time.

- **A verdict looks wrong.** Open a "Wrong verdict" issue from the template.
- **Something is broken, or you have a question.** Open an issue (the "Bug report" or "Feature request" template, or a plain issue for a question).
- **How something works.** Read [README.md](README.md) and the files under [docs/](docs/): verdicts, evidence,
  sources, findings, repairs and safety each have a page.
- **A security problem.** Do not open a public issue; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **A conduct problem.** Follow [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md); do not open a public issue.
- **Contributing a change.** See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## What to put in an issue

- The version (`infirmary --version`, or `stall-explain --version`).
- Your OS and your Python version.
- The exact command you ran.
- The plain output of that command, as text (not a screenshot). If you can, add a redacted evidence snapshot.
- No secrets: remove tokens, keys, passwords and private hostnames or addresses before you paste anything.
